Hey — welcome.

This site is focused on practical, no-fluff guides for building and running a modern homelab in 2026. The goal is simple: help beginners go from zero to a useful, stable setup without drowning in unnecessary complexity.

Most content covers:

- Beginner-friendly homelab setups
- Mini PCs and hardware recommendations
- Proxmox
- Self-hosting useful services (Immich, Vaultwarden, Jellyfin, n8n, etc.)
- Networking and basic security practices

I write from real hands-on experience and try to keep every guide realistic about cost, power consumption, noise, and time investment.

If you find something outdated or incorrect, feel free to reach out.
